01

Dispatch architecture

Weixin Mini Shop audit dispatch platform

Owned the business architecture and core-path implementation for the audit dispatch workbench, spanning task decomposition, workflow orchestration, batch consolidation, routing, and result replay. Rebuilt aggregation and distribution to absorb the campaign surge while linking policy decisions directly to review execution.

~300k QPM

02

Policy intelligence

B-side LLM policy engine

Developed core modules for the merchant-side audit policy engine, grounding rule generation and review decisions in retrieved historical policies and business knowledge. Connected rule configuration, policy execution, and audit scheduling into one executable path, turning governance knowledge into iterable policy rather than static review guidance.

  • Retrieval-grounded rules
  • Policy execution
  • Audit orchestration
03

Risk-control assurance

C-side false-positive review

Designed and implemented a reusable re-review system for order routing and policy interceptions. Added secondary validation, exception fallback, result feedback, and monitoring to close the strategy feedback loop while protecting transaction experience.

Peak review load
~100k QPM
Misflags corrected
~3.7% avg.
