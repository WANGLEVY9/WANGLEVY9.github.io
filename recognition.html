How I work

A note on the service record

Organizing volunteers, running competitions, and keeping student communities active taught a different kind of systems thinking: make responsibility explicit, leave enough context for the next person, and design for the moment when the schedule slips.

Those habits carry directly into research and engineering. A review protocol, a queue, or a handoff is a social interface as much as a technical one.
