Profile

Researcher in formation · Engineer in practice

Building verifiable connections between rigorous inquiry and working systems.

I am an undergraduate in Software Engineering at Nanjing University, conducting research with two software engineering laboratories.

My research interests are Software Testing and Security, AI for Software Engineering, and Trustworthy AI. Beyond whether a model can complete a task, I study how its behavior can be verified, when human judgment should intervene, and how AI-assisted software systems can remain dependable in practice.
