Research profile · October 2026

Making AI-driven software testable, traceable, and accountable.

That question shapes the research I read, the experiments I build, and the production systems I choose to work on.

I am a final-year Software Engineering undergraduate at Nanjing University, working where software testing, GUI agents, and trustworthy AI meet.

My work asks a practical question: once an AI system acts, how do we know whether it acted correctly, where it first went wrong, and when a human should step in? I study controlled agent evaluation, root-cause localization for failed GUI trajectories, and human-in-the-loop inspection under limited budgets. I collaborate with labs at Nanjing University and Nanyang Technological University, and build production systems at Tencent's Weixin Group.
