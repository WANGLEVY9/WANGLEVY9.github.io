A curious engineer with a bias for evidence.

I am an undergraduate Software Engineering student at Nanjing University, working across research labs and production engineering.

My research interests are Software Testing and Security, AI for Software Engineering, and Trustworthy AI. I care about the layer after the demo: evaluation, failure analysis, human oversight, and the operational details that make an intelligent system dependable.
